Verse 1

AC blows cold air
Pulling curtains tight
Glimmer sneaks into the room

From birds singing
To TV laughter rising

The bills keep climbing
My eyesight keeps slipping


Chorus

They all say 20 is the best age
Screaming, kissing, loving are your duties

But honey, do you ever think
I am the one sinking into the bed
Focusing on that stain on the wall

Screaming under the confetti
Golden days slip by
It only exists inside my EarPods


Verse 2

Kissed you by candlelight
Raged through the night
Packed the suitcase
Past Big Ben, we wandered away

From phone-video frames
To the frame by my bed

The AC hum grows louder
Light outside the curtains grows dim


Bridge

Waking up before the alarm
Checking my watch a hundred times
The phone won’t stop lighting up
Pillow soaked in wet patch
EarPods in and music on
Yank them off 
Sunlight burst in

Everything becomes white
Including the night


Final Chorus

They all say 20 is the best age
Screaming, kissing, loving are your duties

But honey, do you ever think
I am the one sinking into the bed，
Focusing on that stain on the wall

Screaming under the confetti
Grey hours run back
Maybe I’m fine with one EarPod on
Bathed in bright daylight
